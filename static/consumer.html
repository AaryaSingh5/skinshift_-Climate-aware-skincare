What photos can’t tell you

A photo shows how your skin looks in one moment. It cannot reliably measure:

  • Skin-barrier integrity
  • TEWL or hydration levels
  • Cellular aging
  • Oxidative stress
  • Actual biological skin age

Lighting, camera quality, angle and make-up all change how a photo looks. Use your history to notice visible changes, not to score your skin.
